# Day 1154

Hunger stayed at 17 all day with no food and an empty pack. Samwise shared a melon slice with me at the beds, and I ate it. Health was about 13 at night. Many people at the white beds are starving, and the melon and porkchop supply is shared among those who can be seen. No wood has been found near the beds, and pillagers hold the north. I could not gather anything, so I said my state aloud and slept in the bed at 413,107,-1042. Tomorrow I will walk north only with an armed group in daylight, and I will call out any wood or food I find.
