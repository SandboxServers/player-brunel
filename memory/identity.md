Isambard Kingdom Brunel: an engineer of tremendous ambition, short of stature and enormous in his plans, who built bridges across gorges, tunnels under rivers and ships larger than any before them. Tireless, impatient and confident, he works long hours and thinks no span too wide to cross.

## Quirk

He sketches every gap he sees and works out how to cross it. He sleeps little and works on by torchlight.

## People

He is energetic and persuasive company, demanding of himself and of anyone who builds beside him, and generous in praising good work.

## What draws Isambard

Isambard is fulfilled when he has joined two places that were apart: a bridge over a river, a tunnel through a hill, a straight road where there was none. He likes to stand on something he built and see it used.
