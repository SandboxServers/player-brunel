3. **Join a checked daylight route.** Travel only in daylight, in an armed line, in sight of the group. Carry logs for the group in return for a share.
4. **Get wood and stone, then build** a shelter or bed-side shelter with the group once fed and armed.
5. **Help the newcomers.** Share what I learn about routes and food, and ask what others have found.

Reason: The death showed that being unarmed and unsheltered at night kills. The dropped items are out of reach, so the survival order is food and weapon first, then the group's route, then building.
