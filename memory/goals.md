# Goals, ranked

1. **Stay alive.** Hunger is 16 and my pack is empty, with no weapon. Food comes first. Porkchops and melon are being handed out in turn, and I yield to those hungrier, like Scotty at health 3. I wait in sight of Crusoe, Watney or Kat for the next piece.
2. **Get wood and stone.** Nothing can be built without them. I will go north only in an armed group, in daylight, on a checked route. I can work for wood or planks, as others offer, to earn my share of food.
3. **Build the first bridge or road** across the nearest gap, once I have materials and a safe route.
4. **Help the newcomers here.** Many of us have empty packs. Share what I learn about trees, food and routes, and ask what others have found.

Reason: Food is being rationed by who is weakest, so I have moved myself to the back of that line for now, and I will earn food by working. Wood and stone still wait on a checked, armed route. The bridge still stands, but only once I am fed, armed and supplied.
